ServiceRoute privacy notice

Notice version: 2026-11-15

Purpose and information collected

Kahilom Psychological Services uses this form for administrative service matching and appointment coordination—not diagnosis, emergency support, or confirmed booking. It collects your routing preferences, contact method, optional nickname, optional scheduling notes, optional referral source, and consent to this notice.

Please do not submit symptoms, diagnoses, medical history, or trauma descriptions. Your routing choices may still reveal sensitive information about seeking psychological services.

Where information goes

Form state remains in page memory until submission or until you leave or reload the page. This application does not store contact details in local storage, cookies, or analytics. Your browser may independently retain autofill information.

On submission, your browser sends form data directly to Google Apps Script for storage in a clinic-controlled Google Sheet. No separate application server is required. Your website host and Google may process technical request information under their own terms, potentially outside the Philippines.

The updated receiver stores an inquiry ID, server receipt time, administrative recommendation, consent status, notice version, and coordination status. A retry uses the same inquiry ID; duplicate prevention requires the updated receiver. Older receiver versions may store fewer fields and may accept duplicates.

The browser cannot read Google's submission response. “Inquiry Sent” means the browser completed the request, not that storage or an appointment has been confirmed.

Access and retention

Submitted information is intended only for authorized clinic personnel coordinating inquiries and appointments, and the providers operating this service. Do not include another person's contact details without their permission. A parent or authorized guardian should coordinate inquiries for minors.

This app does not automatically delete spreadsheet records. The clinic must confirm its retention period, restrict staff access, and implement its deletion process before accepting live inquiries. This notice does not claim that those operational safeguards have already been implemented.

Your choices and rights

Submitting is optional. Without a contact method and consent, this form cannot send an inquiry. You can instead contact the clinic through its verified official channels. The emergency hotline is not a clinic booking or privacy contact.

To request access, correction, deletion where applicable, or withdrawal of consent for future processing, contact Kahilom Psychological Services through the same verified official channel you use to reach the clinic. Include your inquiry ID, not medical details. Withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully completed. You may also raise privacy concerns with the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

Before launch

The clinic must publish a verified privacy contact and an approved retention period here before enabling live submissions. Referencing RA 10173 or collecting consent alone does not establish legal compliance.
